A visual journey through Australia's population growth, cultural diversity, and the migrant stories shaping our communities.

1. Where We Live: Population Density

Australia is one of the most urbanised countries globally, yet its vast interior remains incredibly sparse. This geographic map reveals how our population clusters tightly along the southeastern coastal fringe, driven by historical settlement hubs and climate suitability.

2. Shifting Generations: Population Growth Trend

To understand the trajectory of the "average Australian", we examine how the male and female population segments have expanded over the past four decades. This time-series line chart tracks the parallel growth of both genders, highlighting a steady and balanced upward climb heavily supported by steady net overseas migration.
